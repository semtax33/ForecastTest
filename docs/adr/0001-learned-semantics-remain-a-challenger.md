# Learned semantics remain a challenger

Financial encoders may score concept, metric-to-quantity binding, and quantity-role proposals, but they cannot emit a Research Fact or grant forecast/valuation authority. Narrative structure, exact source spans, cross-clause boundaries, table routing, accounting constraints, and final reduction remain deterministic; low-confidence, unavailable-model, or conflicting proposals abstain. Transformer execution requires a CUDA device when enabled, while the deterministic pipeline remains usable without ML dependencies. This preserves the existing fail-closed safety boundary and avoids coupling valuation behavior to a replaceable model family.
