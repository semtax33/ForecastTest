# Certification uses double-adjudicated gold

Historical parser outputs remain WEAK proposals, automated agreement may produce SILVER data, and one human verifier may produce GOLD_B research data. Only GOLD_A examples—created by two independent annotators and a separate adjudication decision—may enter calibration or certification claims. This costs annotation throughput but prevents the parser author’s assumptions and legacy rule errors from becoming their own evaluation truth.
