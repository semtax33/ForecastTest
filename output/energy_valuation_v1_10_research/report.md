The project-to-reserve effect is identified only at the aggregate disclosed
reserve-investment scope. Component cost shares are reported as evidence, but
no percentage-point ROIC effect is allocated across components because a ratio
of medians is nonlinear. The remaining reserve-to-company gap is retained as
an unexplained residual covering leasehold, shared infrastructure, corporate
overhead, acquisition premium, maintenance/replacement timing, impairment, and
earnings timing. V1.10 does not estimate a normal ROIC, change WACC, or unlock
terminal inputs.
