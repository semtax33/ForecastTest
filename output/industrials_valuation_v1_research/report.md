The primary causal route is Orders/Backlog → Shipments → Revenue → Margin →
Reinvestment → ROIC/FCFF → Valuation. CAT's standardized SEC remaining-
performance-obligation history is used only when an expanding-window
walk-forward regression has at least the predeclared validation sample and
strictly beats the prior-year Revenue baseline on MASE. Otherwise V1 retains
the baseline and labels the anchor locked. This is a research platform, not a
production model or investment recommendation.
